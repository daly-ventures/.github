# Daly Ventures

## Turn high-value workflows into AI systems your teams can trust.

Daly Ventures helps operations and transformation leaders identify valuable AI opportunities, redesign complex workflows, build governed prototypes, and define the controls, adoption plan, and value measures needed for responsible scale.

### What we do

- **AI Strategy & Readiness** — identify and prioritize opportunities by value, feasibility, risk, and organizational readiness.
- **Workflow Design & Automation** — define how people, AI, automation, business rules, evidence, and approval points should work together.
- **AI Adoption & Transformation** — align ownership, governance, enablement, delivery, and measurement so useful systems become part of how work gets done.

### Featured proof: RealInsight

RealInsight demonstrates a governed AI-assisted decision workflow for first-time homebuyers.

It keeps source evidence visible, preserves unknowns, requires human confirmation, and uses deterministic application rules for consequential boundaries. The model supports the decision without owning it.

[View the source-visible prototype](https://github.com/brdaly/RealInsight) · [Launch the Rules demo](https://realinsight-openai.brendandaly.chatgpt.site)

**Current status:** Public portfolio demonstration. Live AI is opt-in; the complete deterministic workflow remains available in clearly labeled Rules mode. RealInsight is not real-estate, appraisal, lending, legal, or financial advice.

### How we build

**Interpret → Ground → Govern → Approve**

AI may interpret ambiguity. Evidence remains reviewable. Code and policy control hard rules. People retain authority over consequential decisions.
